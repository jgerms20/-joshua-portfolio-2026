03 — How it was built

How it was built

  • AI toolsBuilt with Codex.
  • ChecksAn automated test suite and a production build, both run by the daily refresh before it publishes.
  • Daily refreshA scheduled GitHub Actions job refreshes the catalog every day. It keeps existing records, adds newly found source-linked posts up to the catalog cap, refreshes the curated X reaction media, runs the full test suite and the build, commits only the data that changed, and publishes the checked build to Vercel.

04 — What it does

What it does

It finds the exact meme, reaction image, GIF, video or cultural clip you half remember. You can search by quote, mood, clothing, visual detail, community, platform or context, then look at the media, its verified source links, its spread timeline and its lifecycle.

  • Recall searchNatural-language search across more than 1,000 source-linked records.
  • MediaReal images and GIFs, privacy-enhanced YouTube playback, curated reaction video served from the site, and limited fallbacks to public X posts.
  • BrowsePlatform, media and community filters, plus Trending and Saved views.
  • ProvenanceEach record links its platform, creator, first upload, when it was captured and whether it is still up.
  • RemovalRights holders and people featured can report media or request removal from the site.
  • InterfaceLight and dark themes, on desktop and mobile.

05 — Its limits

Its limits

  • RightsMost media stays on its public source host. The 14 curated X reaction clips are copied onto the site so they play reliably, and keep their original post and creator links. There is no formal rights-review queue yet; a larger release should add one.
  • AttributionSome source accounts are archive accounts that curate memes rather than the people who made them, so the linked "creator" is not always the original creator.
  • IngestionThere is no server-side multimodal ingestion pipeline yet, and the catalog has a cap on how many records it holds.
