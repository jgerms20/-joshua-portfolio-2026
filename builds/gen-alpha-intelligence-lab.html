04 — What it does

What it does

It maps Gen Alpha signals and how they contrast with Gen Z, together with the source evidence and interviews behind them.

  • Signal mapOpens on an insight overview and a map of the signals the Lab is tracking.
  • Research recordsTracks records across reports, articles, podcasts, interviews and field notes.
  • FiltersSearch, then narrow by type, status and tag.
  • IntakeAn intake form adds new sources or interviews, with file upload for transcripts, source documents, and audio or video interviews.
