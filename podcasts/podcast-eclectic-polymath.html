02 The territory

No lane. No niche.

Whatever’s too interesting to ignore usually lands in one of these.

  1. 01

    Power & Crime

    Drug cartels, criminal empires, and the economics of power that runs outside the law.

  2. 02

    Ancient & Religious History

    Prophecy, myth, and the belief systems that shaped civilizations and still quietly shape ours.

  3. 03

    Political Dynasties

    How concentrated power gets built, inherited, and lost, from California to empires past.

  4. 04

    Psychology & Persuasion

    The science of influence, the psychology of advertising, and why people decide the way they do.

  5. 05

    Historical Revolutions

    The moments systems collapsed and got rebuilt, and what the people inside them lived through.

  6. 06

    Geopolitics & Culture

    Where nations, identities, and cultures collide, and what is actually driving the world right now.

  7. 07

    Science & Human Biology

    What we know about ourselves, from evolutionary psychology to the science most of us never got in school.

  8. 08

    Black History & Identity

    The stories that shaped Black culture, politics, and identity, told with the depth they deserve.

03 From the host

Every episode starts with a question I couldn’t put down.

Cartels and dynasties. Prophecy and myth. Why an ad works on you. I do the research, follow the thread as deep as it goes, and come back with the story underneath.

There’s no lane and no niche here. Just whatever is too interesting to ignore. I host, produce, and edit every episode myself.

Joshua McKenzie GermanLos Angeles, CA
