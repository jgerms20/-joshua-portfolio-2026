    02 The territory

    Everything in between.

    Crypto, politics, sports, music, movies. The conversations kept coming back to these.

    1. 01

      Crypto & the New Economy

      Blockchain, NFTs as a new medium for creators, the Metaverse as an economic platform, and why staying current with Web3 matters.

    2. 02

      Politics & Power

      The Capitol, the Georgia runoffs, the two-party system, and politics from the local level up.

    3. 03

      Sports

      NBA madness, the Black quarterback in NFL history, and what it takes to stay dominant on the gridiron.

    4. 04

      Film & Documentary

      13th, Just Mercy, Crash, Green Book, and what the screen says about the country we live in.

    5. 05

      Music & Ownership

      Independent artists, making an album, owning your work, and Master P’s rise to mogul status.

    6. 06

      Building Something

      Opening a restaurant, launching a clothing brand, real estate, budgeting, and building a Black-owned economy.

    7. 07

    8. 08

    03 Around the table

    Strive to dominate the decade in all aspects of life.

    Adarius Simpkins, Matthew Anderson, and Josh German opened the show with “The Intro” in June 2020, in the middle of the protests for George Floyd, Breonna Taylor, and Ahmaud Arbery, talking about what it would take to build a media empire.

    From there it ran through 2023: documentaries and box scores, stimulus checks and blockchains, year-in-review episodes, and guests who pulled up a chair along the way.
