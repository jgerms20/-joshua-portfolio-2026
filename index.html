Hey, I’m Joshua. Curious and creative, and happiest telling stories in whatever medium fits.

Born and raised in South Carolina, and I’ve always loved connecting with people: seeing them, hearing them, figuring out what makes them tick. That pulled me into journalism, then advertising, where I’m a strategist working out where, how, and most importantly why an idea reaches people.

I’m also a podcaster and producer, because I love a deep conversation. I’ve been all in on AI since the ChatGPT moment: building tools, testing everything, and helping brands keep up. With Janel Moore I run Curious Creative Company, a podcast network and creative studio that grew out of the same itch.

And I shoot. People, places, nights out, the moments in between. Most of what you’ll find here started as a question I couldn’t leave alone. Look around, and say hi.
