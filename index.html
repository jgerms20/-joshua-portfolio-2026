I started as a reporter. I still work like one — find the real story, then make people feel it.

Columbia, South Carolina. Journalism & Mass Communications at the University of South Carolina, a minor in Information Science, and a run as assistant sports editor at The Daily Gamecock. Deadlines taught me the whole job: find the story, get it right, file it before midnight.

Then advertising. Goodby Silverstein & Partners, Wieden+Kennedy, and now TBWA\Chiat\Day, where I’m a Senior Connections Strategist on Levi’s, Sephora, and DIRECTV. My job is deciding where a brand shows up — and making sure it earns the attention once it gets there. Some of that work ran in the Super Bowl. Some of it won at Cannes.

I also build. For four years I’ve shipped my own tools with Claude, Codex, and Gemini — audience intelligence systems, strategy apps, interactive experiences. A strategy is easier to believe when you can click on it.

Off the clock I shoot, host, and write. Behind the lens or behind the mic, it’s the same instinct: understand the thing, then tell it better than I found it.
