Chapter One — Columbia, South Carolina

Origin

It started in Columbia. Before the campaigns, before the Grand Prix, before the machines — there was a kid at the University of South Carolina learning that stories are the only currency that matters. Journalism school taught the fundamentals: get the fact right, find the human, earn the read.

At The Daily Gamecock, that instinct sharpened into craft. Writing on deadline, editing for clarity, chasing stories that connected to something real. The tools would change. The discipline never would.

Columbia was the foundation. Everything else is built on it.

Chapter Two — The Rise

Charlotte → SF

Charlotte became home base. GSP — a creative agency punching above its weight — became the proving ground. Here the journalism muscles converted into brand thinking: instead of covering stories, Joshua was building them, from brief to broadcast.

The client roster sharpened instincts fast: BMW demanded precision and aspiration in the same breath; Samuel Adams and Xfinity asked for culture at scale. The work traveled from Charlotte to San Francisco, coast to coast, getting sharper with every campaign. The industry was paying attention.

Chapter Three — Portland

Wieden+Kennedy

Portland, Oregon · Global Creative

The call to Portland. Wieden+Kennedy — the agency that made Nike say "Just Do It," that put Old Spice on the cultural map — was where Joshua's strategic voice found its full register. Portland is where good work becomes legendary work.

The DoorDash "All The Ads" Super Bowl campaign was a masterclass in structured chaos — a real-time sweepstakes that gave away every product advertised during the Big Game. America noticed. The industry gave it the highest honour it knows.

Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity Grand Prix DoorDash "All The Ads" — Direct

One Grand Prix. The kind of recognition that validates not just the work, but the approach: that creativity and strategy, when aligned with genuine cultural insight, can change what people believe is possible in advertising.
