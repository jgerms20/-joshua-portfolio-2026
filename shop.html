How Printing Works

Print & Framing.

Every print ships from a print-on-demand lab once an order is confirmed — nothing is printed until you've said yes to the final price. Framing, when you choose it, comes from the same lab so the piece arrives ready to hang.

Paper

Archival fine-art matte, 100% cotton rag. The same stock giclée print shops use for gallery work — no gloss, no color shift over time.

Framing

Optional solid-wood frame in black, white, or natural oak, with UV-protective glazing. Added as a line item at checkout — nothing ships unframed by accident.

Shipping

Rolled in a tube (unframed) or boxed flat (framed), insured, with tracking. Framed pieces run 7–10 days; unframed prints ship faster.
