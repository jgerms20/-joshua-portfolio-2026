## Transferable patterns for Joshua (partly inferred; labeled)
1. **Lead with a specialty plus place, not "photographer."** Observed: Tahlier's slug (Chicago food photographer), Mueller's "tabletop director in New York." Joshua: "Los Angeles photographer of people, culture and the places they gather" style line.
2. **Name real clients and publications as proof.** Observed: Rucker (Runner's World, Anthropologie), Tahlier (Alinea, Intelligentsia), Mueller (Snickers, Halo Top). Joshua: use events, brands, and publications he has actually shot; if few, use series titles.
3. **Group by series or story, not just category.** Observed: Knight's "Day in the Life" and named projects. Each series gets a title, a one-line premise and a handful of frames.
4. **Case-study posts per job.** Observed: Mueller's news subdomain. Joshua: short posts (brief, setting, result, credits) that double as SEO and as proof of process.
5. **Pair a strategist angle with the photos.** General practice: his edge is that he thinks about why the image works. Add one line of intent per series.
6. **Combine About and Contact on one page.** Observed: Rucker's /about-contact URL. Short bio, then how to book.
7. **Make booking explicit.** Rep-routed for Rucker and Knight (observed). Joshua has no rep, so: a form or email, a short list of shoot types, and a "what to send me" prompt. Rates: general practice is to offer a request-a-quote line, not a price list.
8. **Location+specialty landing pages for search.** Inferred from Tahlier's slug. Joshua: a page each for LA portrait, fashion/editorial, event, travel.
9. **Let the proof be the images.** Images dominate; text is short captions and credits (general practice for photographers; not observed directly).
10. **Show range through categories but lead with one.** Tahlier and Zarebinski read as still-life/food specialists; Knight as lifestyle. Joshua's list is broad, so pick a lead (portrait/editorial) and show others as secondary tiles.
11. **Awards and press as small trust marks** (Mueller's IPA 2021 post).
12. **Warm, human tone as a brand.** Knight's stated goal (feel good, warm, celebrated) is a usable model for Joshua's own one-line promise.

## Recommended structure: story-led photography microsite
1. **Home** - one hero image, one-line positioning, 3 to 4 series tiles (title plus one-line premise), client/publication strip, Book button.
2. **Series pages** (4-6) - title, premise paragraph, 8-14 images, credits (client, role, location, year), "shoot something like this" CTA.
3. **Services** - portraits, fashion/editorial, events, travel; product, food, spaces as "also". Each with who it's for, what you get, turnaround.
4. **Journal/case studies** (optional) - brief, approach, result.
5. **About + Contact** - first person, 150-250 words: where he works, how a creative strategist sees, one proof line. Then form plus email, response time, what to include (date, location, usage, budget range).
6. Cross-link to the main portfolio so the strategy and photo sides support each other.
