# Photographer site research, batch A (2026-11-14)

Sites: lucaszarebinski.com, secretstan.co, andrerucker.com, amueller.com, iheartreps.com.
Method: curl + Playwright at 1440px, HTML saved under the session scratchpad `sites-a/`. Quotes are verbatim.

Limits to know first:
- The sandbox blocks the image/CSS CDNs (images.squarespace-cdn.com, static1.squarespace.com, static.photofolio.com, lkbkspro.s3). Screenshots therefore show no photos, and three of them (Lucas, Stan, Andre) render unstyled. Layout and type notes for those come from markup, JSON and metadata, not from looking at the finished page. Only Adrian Mueller's splash and the I Heart Reps hero rendered properly.
- Bertram Knight is NOT on iheartreps.com. The roster (below) has no such name, `/photographers-directors/bertram-knight` returns 404, and the string does not appear in any fetched page. A web search found nothing usable (one stock-image credit, "Bertram Knight, Photographer, New York", no portfolio). Joshua may have the agency or name wrong; no personal site could be found.

## 10 patterns for Joshua to borrow

1. **Say who, where, what, availability in one visible line.** Stan: "award-winning photographer, video producer, and visual storyteller specializing in food, travel, hospitality, and events. Available for commissions in Los Angeles and worldwide." For Joshua: "Los Angeles photographer: portraits, fashion and editorial, event coverage, travel. Booking worldwide." Lucas and Andre have no such line and rely on metadata; that is the weaker choice.
2. **A booking verb in the nav, not "Contact".** Stan's "Book a Shoot Now" in the nav, a repeat "Let's create!" button, and both go to the same form. Joshua's studio form is the CTA; label it "Book a shoot" and repeat it at the end of each page.
3. **Make the form tiny and say so.** Stan's form is two fields: Email (required) + "Tell me about your project". Joshua's studio form can keep its existing fields, but the page should state the ask in a sentence ("Tell me about your project by filling out the form below or send me an email at...") and give a plain reply promise ("I'll get back to you as soon as possible").
4. **Group by what the buyer is hiring for.** Lucas (Cosmetics / Food / Textures / Motion) and Adrian (beverage / food / product / motion / commissions) use category names a client would search. For Joshua: Portraits, Fashion & Editorial, Events, Travel as the four top-level groups. Skip invented names like "Specular" unless a series truly earns one.
5. **Project pages as short commissions.** Stan's template: title, "Location: Santa Monica, CA", who commissioned, what the occasion was, one sentence on the lighting/approach, 20 or so images, Previous/Next. This is the best fit for event and travel coverage; add a "Who / Where / When / Delivered" mini-line.
6. **Credit line = client or outlet.** Andre captions in caps ("URBAN OUTFITTERS", "WIRED", "WASHINGTON POST"); Lucas uses brand + product line. One short caption per frame beats filenames. Fix the Lucas flaw: hide filenames from alt text.
7. **A client strip near the end, plain text, alphabetised.** Lucas and Adrian use text lists; Stan uses a logo wall with the heading "Loved by Teams at". Do text first (fast, accessible). Add magazines separately, as Adrian does.
8. **A dated recognition list, not an adjective.** Andre's CV lines ("'DNA' - SPD 60, Gold - Conceptual/Still Life '25") and Adrian's "featured in Lurzer's Archive ... 16 consecutive years". Joshua should list only what he can verify: publication, award or festival, year.
9. **About page: one tight paragraph that leads with a fact, then place and range.** Stan leads with employers and reach, Adrian with a stat then origin story, Andre with a childhood line. Keep it under about 150 words; put the contact block directly under it (Andre, Adrian do this) so there is no second click.
10. **Show where you shoot and travel from.** Adrian: "available to shoot in Chicago and Los Angeles"; Stan: "Los Angeles and worldwide... from Dubai to Louisville to Taipei"; I Heart Reps: "Based in New York City and Los Angeles / With artists located and available worldwide." Joshua's contact line should read: "Based in Los Angeles. Travels for work."

Bonus from I Heart Reps: a per-artist "Download PDF" and a category filter ("Portrait", "Fashion", "Lifestyle", "Landscape & Travel") are worth a lightweight version (a one-page "work with me" PDF with rates or a service list; Stan also publishes a "rate sheet").

## Selling vocabulary actually used (grouped)

**Who you are.** award-winning; visual storyteller; photographer and director ("photographer & director"); still life photographer and tabletop director; video producer; creative; "favorite among diverse clients"; "at the forefront of"; "mastery of light"; "conceptualization and artistic direction"; "Beauty in Simplicity"; "culturally defining artists"; "visionary brands".

**What you deliver.** commissioned to capture; campaigns; national campaigns; advertising imagery; commercial; editorial commissions; content; launch; anniversary; cookbooks; events; hospitality; travel; motion; stills.

**How the work looks.** rich, directional lighting; high-contrast lighting; refined; elegant; polished; clean architectural approach; minimal; graphic; heroic; tactile; precise; authentic and appetizing; "mouthwatering"; "texture, color, and flavor"; "gloss and grain"; "fine art sensibility"; surrealism; conceptual.

**How you work.** "organized, calm leadership" (Adrian's caption: "Clients appreciate his organized, calm leadership..."); "collaborating with clients to bring their vision to life"; "working alongside talented creatives and teams"; "nurtures their craft"; "long-term partner"; "repeat partnerships".

**Proof.** "over 15 years"; "over 80 million shares and views"; "16 consecutive years"; James Beard; Cannes Lions; Clio; The One Show; Lurzer's 200 Best; PDN; "from startups to industry leading luxury brands"; "Loved by Teams at"; "Selected Clients".

**Reach and availability.** "Available worldwide"; "Available for commissions in Los Angeles and worldwide"; "available to shoot in Chicago and Los Angeles"; "Based in the heart of Los Angeles"; "offices in New York City and Los Angeles".

**Calls to action.** "Book a Shoot Now"; "Let's create!"; "Tell me about your project"; "Say Hello"; "REACH OUT"; "Now Representing"; "Download PDF"; "Join my mailing list"; "Indie non-corporate rate sheet available here (for small business)".

**Things to avoid copying.** Adrian's "Best ... Photographer - NYC" keyword captions and Stan's four-paragraph SEO block read as search bait, not as a person. Lucas's broken newsletter block and image filenames in alt text are cautionary examples.
